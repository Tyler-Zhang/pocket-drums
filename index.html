Setup & timing

Connect the Roland module to your Mac by USB and select its MIDI input. Use a browser with Web MIDI, such as Chrome. Install the module’s driver if your Mac requires it. Grant MIDI access when prompted.

To follow the V51, open SYSTEM MIDI / SYNC on the module and set Sync Mode to INTERNAL and Sync Out to ON. Connect MIDI here, choose Follow MIDI clock, the trainer follows automatically. MIDI Start aligns bar 1. Continue resumes, and Stop pauses. If the module sends clock alone, select Align beat 1, then strike one pad on the accented V51 click. Hit scoring starts once the grid is aligned. Playground audio playback starts with a tap.

With App tempo, Start gives you a one-bar count-in. Each hit is compared with the nearest sixteenth note, including rests. This measures timing only. Positive offset subtracts input/audio latency from hit timestamps. Use wired headphones and adjust until hits you hear on the click center on zero.
